Topic modeling discovers themes in a collection of documents without labels. A modern pipeline embeds each document, reduces the dimensionality of the vectors with a method such as UMAP, and groups nearby points with a density based algorithm such as HDBSCAN, which also marks outliers as noise instead of forcing them into a cluster. To make the clusters interpretable, a weighting scheme similar to TF-IDF is applied across each cluster to find the words that best distinguish it from the others. The cluster labels can then be refined by asking a language model to summarize the top words and representative documents. Clustering helps researchers explore a large corpus, spot duplicates, and decide which categories a classifier should predict. Results are sensitive to the embedding model and to hyperparameters like minimum cluster size.
